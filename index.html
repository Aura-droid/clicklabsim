Screen 02 Bio-Orthogonal Cellular Sandbox

Why did click chemistry win the 2022 Nobel Prize? Inject fluorescent SPAAC probes into a living cell and watch them ignore every native protein — and click only onto the azide-tagged target.

Why it matters

CuAAC and SPAAC are bio-orthogonal: they proceed inside living systems without disturbing native biochemistry. The strained cyclooctyne (DBCO) reacts with azides faster than any biological functional group can — no copper, no toxicity — which is exactly why this chemistry earned Sharpless, Meldal & Bertozzi the 2022 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

Every gray particle the probe drifts past without reacting is a demonstration of selectivity.
